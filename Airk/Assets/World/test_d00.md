# Maintenance Tunnel

**short:** A cramped tunnel beneath the alley, lit by emergency strips.

**description:**
The tunnel is barely tall enough to stand in. Red *emergency strips* run
along the ceiling, casting everything in a dim crimson glow. *Cables* and
conduit line the walls. The air smells of damp concrete and machine oil.
