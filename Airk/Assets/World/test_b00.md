# Neon Boulevard

**short:** A wide street buzzing with neon signs and drone traffic.

**description:**
The alley spills out onto a broad *boulevard* choked with light and noise.
Holographic *advertisements* tower above, cycling through neural upgrades,
synth-food deals, and NeoCortex public safety messages. Drones buzz overhead.
Pedestrians weave past in a hurry, eyes down, interfaces glowing at their temples.
