# Rooftop

**short:** A flat rooftop overlooking the alley below.

**description:**
Wind whips across the exposed *rooftop*. The city sprawl stretches in every
direction, towers of glass and neon piercing the smog layer. A rusted
*satellite dish* tilts at an angle near the edge.
